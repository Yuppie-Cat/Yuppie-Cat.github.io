## Tom Zhou

I am a PhD student in the Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at **Case Western Reserve University (CWRU)**, located in Cleveland, Ohio. I work in the [Biologically Inspired Robotics Lab](https://engineering.case.edu/emae/research/biologically-inspired-robotics) under the supervision of Prof. Kathryn Daltorio.

My research sits at the intersection of **robotics, sensing, and biomechanics**, with a focus on enabling legged robots to perceive and interact with complex, unstructured environments. I am particularly interested in developing miniaturized force sensors that can be embedded directly into robot appendages — feet, toes, and hands — to provide rich tactile feedback without sacrificing mechanical performance.

---

## Research Interests

- **Force Sensing for Legged Robots** — Designing compact, high-load-density sensors for robot feet and end effectors
- **Terrain Adaptation** — Using proprioceptive feedback to classify terrain types and detect buried objects
- **Robotic Manipulation** — Applying distal force sensing to manipulation tasks in unstructured environments
- **Bio-inspired Robotics** — Drawing from biological systems to inform robot design and control
